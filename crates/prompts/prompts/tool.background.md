Read or stop a background command. `action` is `list`, `read`, `wait` or `stop`. `list` shows every process of the Session; `read` takes `id` and an `offset` into the log and answers with the chunk and the next offset; `wait` takes `id` and `timeout` in seconds (default 60, max 600) and answers exited or timeout; `stop` takes `id` and ends the process group.
